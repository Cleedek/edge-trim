    Trimming

    Nearly invisible pixels

    Cuts right after the last pixel that isn’t fully transparent.

    Also cuts away haze that’s almost invisible (under 2% opacity), often left behind by background removal.

    Also cuts away soft shadows and glow under 10% opacity. Check the result: the edge of the object itself can get cut too.

    When you download a PNG, WebP, AVIF or GIF from any website (also after a countdown or a captcha), it’s replaced by a version trimmed with the setting above, under the same name. Pictures with nothing to trim stay as they are.

    On a web page, right-click any picture and choose "Trim and copy image" or "Trim and save image", or right-click a download link and choose "Trim and download linked image". Everything is trimmed on this computer, nothing is uploaded.
